# 📊 Executive Briefing: Constituency #5 Voter Profile Analysis

We analyzed voting data across **293 polling stations** using machine learning.  
Unlike previous constituencies, this region shows a **strong three-way contest**:

- **Congress (INC)** → Secure fortress  
- **TVK** → Dominant in swing zones  
- **AIADMK** → Strong comeback with stable base

## 🟢 1. Congress Absolute Fortress (21 Booths — Cluster 0)

**The Numbers:**
- INC: 60.87%
- TVK: 20.15%
- AIADMK: 12.54%

**The Insight:**  
A **secure ideological stronghold** with a massive **39.98% margin**.  
No meaningful impact from opposition or independents.

**🎯 Action Items:**
- **INC:** Maintain engagement and ensure strong turnout  
- **Others:** Avoid resource allocation — low conversion probability  

---

## ⚔️ 2. High-Stakes Battleground: TVK vs AIADMK (162 Booths — Cluster 1)

**The Numbers:**
- TVK: 37.49%
- AIADMK: 28.81%
- INC: 23.83%
- NTK: 7.63%

**The Insight:**  
This is the **largest segment (55% of booths)** and the **decisive battleground**.  
Margin is extremely tight (**~8.70%**), with no clear dominance.

**🎯 Action Items:**
- Highest campaign priority  
- Focus on **NTK and undecided voters**  
- A **~3% vote swing** can flip results across all 162 booths

## 🪵 4. Puthiya Tamilagam Impact Zone (21 Booths — Cluster 3)

**The Numbers:**
- TVK: 35.72%
- AIADMK: 30.93%

- Even small shifts can change results
