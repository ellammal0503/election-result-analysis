# 🗳️ Kolathur Election Result Analysis (Booth-Level)

This module provides a **detailed booth-wise forensic analysis** of election results for the **Kolathur Constituency**, with a focus on **DMK loss patterns and contributing factors**.

---

## 📊 Constituency Summary

- **DMK Vote Share:** 40.34% (73,220 votes)  
- **TVK Vote Share:** 45.51% (82,614 votes)  
- **AIADMK Vote Share:** 10.04% (18,215 votes)  

---
## 🔬 Special Analysis

### 📍 Kolathur – DMK Loss Forensic Analysis
- DMK lost 197 out of 286 booths  
- Vote splitting impacted 54 booths  
- TVK dominated non-DMK booths  

📄 Detailed report available in:
`kolathur/data/processed/DMK_Loss_Analysis.xlsx`

## 📉 Key Findings

- DMK lost in **197 out of 286 polling stations**  
- **TVK dominated**, winning all 197 booths where DMK lost  

---

## 🔍 Vote Splitting Analysis

- In **54 polling stations**, DMK lost by a margin **smaller than the votes secured by Minor Parties & Independents**  
- Indicates **vote splitting played a critical role** in the overall result
