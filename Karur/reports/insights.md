# 🗳️ Booth-Level Election Analysis – Strategic ML Insights (Tamil Nadu 2026)

## 📌 Overview

This project presents a **machine learning–driven electoral analysis** at the booth level.
Using clustering techniques, we identify **micro-level voting patterns**, battleground zones, and strongholds across constituencies.

---

## 📊 Key Insight

Unlike previous datasets with dominant single-party zones, this dataset reveals:

* ⚔️ **87% of booths are highly competitive**
* 📉 **Single-digit victory margins dominate**
* 🔄 **Minor vote swings can change outcomes**

---

## 🧠 ML Approach

* Algorithm: **K-Means Clustering**
* Features:

  * Party vote share %
  * Margin of victory
* Goal:

  * Identify **behavioral voting segments**
  * Enable **data-driven campaign strategy**
